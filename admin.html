.env Authentication & Admin Security

Credentials are dynamically loaded from your root .env file.

Environment-Based Recovery Guarantee

As configured, your admin username and password reside directly in the .env file. If you ever forget your password, you will NEVER be locked out: simply open the .env file in Notepad on your computer, view or change ADMIN_PASSWORD=your_new_password, save the file, and you can log in immediately!
